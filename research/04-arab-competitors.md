# 04 — Arab-World Competitors & Ideas (GCC, Levant, North Africa)

**Research angle:** Building, community, owners-association and home-services platforms in Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain, Jordan, Lebanon, Morocco and Tunisia. We look at what they do, how they make money, how far they have got, what makes them strong, and what an Egyptian building app can borrow. A dedicated section covers **regulation-driven adoption** and whether Egypt has an equivalent lever.
**Research date:** 2026-10-12
**Target market of the client:** Egypt only. Everything below is studied *to borrow ideas*. None of these players is a direct competitor in Egypt today.

> Conventions: every factual claim carries a source URL. **ESTIMATE** marks my own reasoning or numbers. "Not found" means I searched and could not verify the point. Company press releases and vendor blogs are self-reported, and the text says so where it matters.

## 0. Executive summary: the 12 things that matter most

1. **In the Gulf, mass adoption of building/community platforms came from regulation, not from marketing.** Saudi Arabia's **Mullak (مُلّاك)** and Dubai's **Mollak** grew because owners' associations and service-charge billing were made legally mandatory and routed through a government platform. Saudi Mullak reached ~36,000 associations, 126,000+ active members and 160,000+ units by end-2023 ([REGA](https://rega.gov.sa/media-center/الأخبار-والإعلانات/الهيئة-العامة-للعقار-أكثر-من-مليون-عملية-عبر-منصة-م-لاك-حتى-نهاية-2023م/)). Saudi Ejar passed 10M lease contracts once paying rent through it became compulsory ([REGA](https://rega.gov.sa/en/media-center/news-announcements/ejar-registers-over-10-million-rental-contracts-since-launch/); [SPA](https://www.spa.gov.sa/N2023052)).
2. **Egypt has a similar lever, but it is weak and only partly activated.** Building Law 119/2008 (Part 4) provides for **اتحاد الشاغلين** (occupants' unions). They gain legal personality on registration at the local unit, and the threshold is 5+ units per Art. 72 ([youm7 2025](https://www.youm7.com/story/2025/12/29/4-%D9%85%D9%87%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%B1%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%BA%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%81%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D9%8A%D9%88%D8%B6%D8%AD/7248456); [law 119 text](https://unionbuildingsagouzadistrict.wordpress.com/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-119/)). **The Ministry of Housing is actively drafting amendments to the occupants'-union chapter.** The Minister reviewed them on **3 Aug 2026** and ordered the final drafting finished quickly. The amendments cover formation procedures, how to deal with buildings that do not form a union, and the developer's maintenance responsibilities ([Hapi Journal](https://hapijournal.com/2026/08/03/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%B3%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%B4-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8/); [Followict, 15 Jan 2026](https://followict.news/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%B3%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%B1%D8%AD-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%AF/)). **This is the biggest free distribution opportunity found in this research.** An app that makes union formation, registration, minutes, budgets and dues compliant, close to "one-click", could become the de-facto tool when the amendments pass.
3. **Gulf B2B community apps (ADDA, NoBrokerHood, ISKAAN, Anacity, HappyTenant, Facilio) sell to management companies, not to residents.** They win through Mollak integration, gate/visitor control and billing ([Facilio list](https://facilio.com/blog/best-residential-community-management-platforms-in-uae/); [Gulf News on ADDA](https://gulfnews.com/amp/business/property/connected-communities-use-adda-to-enrich-resident-experience-in-the-uae-1.1619593114152)). Egypt's ordinary buildings (عمارات) have **no professional manager**, so a **resident-first, free** app fits Egypt better. Compounds are the exception, because they are managed by developers or facility-management (FM) firms.
4. **The exact "free adoption, then monetize" playbook already works at scale, and it came from India into the UAE.** MyGate offers a free or low-cost model to drive adoption and then monetizes through **advertising (largest line)** plus SaaS. It claims 27,000+ communities and ~5.7M households (company claim in its June 2026 ₹225 Cr Dharana Capital round; Inc42's FY25 piece says "nearly 5 Mn homes") [corrected: source], with FY25 operating revenue of ₹173.5 Cr (+80%), advertising ~65–70% of income, and break-even before ESOP costs ([YourStory Jun 2026](https://www.yourstory.com/2026/06/mygate-raises-rs-225-cr-from-dharana-capital); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/mygate-trims-fy25-loss-by-61-to-₹15-4-cr/); [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/fintrackr/mygate-total-revenue-nears-rs-110-cr-in-fy24-losses-shrink-by-82-7374229)). NoBrokerHood (same model, Mollak-integrated) is now in the UAE ([nobrokerhood.com/ae](https://nobrokerhood.com/ae)).
5. **Pure home-services marketplaces in the Gulf are hard. Several pivoted, shrank or exited.** Munjz (KSA) began as a B2C maintenance marketplace in 2017 and **pivoted to B2B PropTech SaaS in Nov 2021** ([Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2023/02/munjz-closes-4-8million-SeriesA-funding-round-arabic-english)). Urban Company **wound down its Saudi subsidiary (May 2026)** and runs Saudi Arabia through a 50:50 JV with SMASCO under the "Yammak" brand ([Indian Retailer](https://indianretailer.com/news/urban-company-shifts-saudi-operations-joint-venture)). The lesson: **own the household relationship first, then add services.** That is exactly the client's strategy, and it is the opposite of what failed.
6. **The Gulf winner in services is Justlife.** It reports 10M+ bookings, 55% YoY growth in core verticals, and >75% of 2025 revenue from returning users ([Zawya PR](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/justlife-crosses-10mln-bookings-strengthening-its-role-in-the-uaes-service-economy-snkn0ebt)). Its distribution came partly from **embedding inside Careem's super-app**, where >70% of home-service bookings come from Careem Plus members ([Careem blog, Aug 2025](https://blog.careem.com/posts/more-home-services-now-on-careem); TBreak link now 404). Its **Trustpilot score is 2.7/5**. Complaints are about no-shows, last-minute cancellations, and no phone support ([Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/justlife.com)). **Reliability is the unsolved problem in this category.**
7. **ServiceMarket (UAE) was acquired by e& in 2023 and folded into the Smiles super-app** ([ServiceMarket](https://servicemarket.com/en/about-us); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/movesouq)). This shows the exit path for a services aggregator: become a vertical inside a telco or super-app. In Egypt, that suggests an eventual partner or acquirer such as a telco or a payments super-app (**ESTIMATE / strategic inference**).
8. **Developer-branded community apps (Emaar One, Azizi VCM, ROSHN's Community App) are standard in the Gulf.** They offer service-charge payment, service requests (Emaar has **voice** requests), amenity booking, move-in permits, NOCs and home-service bookings ([Emaar](https://uae.emaar.com/en/blog/manage-your-emaar-property-and-household-efficiently-with-emaar-one); [ECM](https://www.ecm.ae/en/communities/rashid-yachts-marina/lifestyle/faqs/top-10-questions-2)). Egyptian compounds will expect this level. A white-label "powered by" offer to Egyptian developers is a credible B2B lane.
9. **The WhatsApp group-bot idea (founder idea #3) runs into hard platform limits.** Meta's official **Groups API caps a group at 8 participants**, is **invite-only**, needs an **Official Business Account**, and **cannot manage existing groups** ([Meta docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/groups/); [Periskope](https://periskope.app/blog/whatsapp-groups-api-requirements-eligibility-limits)). Since **15 Jan 2026**, Meta has banned **general-purpose** AI assistants on the Business API. Task-specific bots such as bookings and support are still allowed ([respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-general-purpose-chatbots-ban); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/no-more-chatgpt-and-perplexity-on-whatsapp-meta-bans-major-ai-chatbots-from-2026-1.500316016)). A bot that "joins the existing 80-person building group" is therefore **not possible through official APIs**. Unofficial automation risks number bans. Section 7 gives compliant designs.
10. **Government WhatsApp AI works in the region when it is task-scoped.** DEWA's Rammas answered 219,000 WhatsApp questions and did 5,800+ bill payments by Dec 2020 (4M+ total enquiries 2017–Dec 2020), and 6.8M enquiries overall by end-2022 [6.8M unverified] ([Dubai Media Office, Feb 2021](https://mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2021/Feb/02-02/DEWAs-AI-services) [corrected: source]; [DEWA 2023 PR](https://feeds.dfm.ae/documents/2023/Mar/01/65b22f5e-aa5c-4164-82ed-8c7619b6e9c3/DEWA_PR_E_1_03_2023.pdf)). The model to copy is a 1:1 WhatsApp assistant scoped to building tasks such as "pay my dues", "report a leak" or "book a plumber".
11. **Collection of dues is the #1 pain everywhere in the region**, and it is the hook for daily or monthly engagement. Examples: Dubai service-charge collection fell ~15% after the new law and Mollak, with >50% shortfalls in some funds ([CMS](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/Service-Charges-a-tug-of-war-between-management-companies-and-property-owners)). Morocco's association calls dues recovery "a major problem" ([FNH](https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/copropriete-reforme-reglementation)). "Intermediate" syndics in Tunisia collect only ~75% ([La Presse/AllAfrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202608170324.html)). Saudi Arabia solved it with an **executive bond (سند تنفيذي)** issued via Mullak and Najiz ([Al-Eqtisadiah](https://www.aleqt.com/2024/09/09/article_2749638.html); [Raghdan](https://raghdan.sa/ar/news/homeowners-association-guide-saudi-2025/)). **Transparency, reminders and social proof are what Egypt can copy without a law.**
12. **No Arab player owns the resident-first building app for ordinary (non-managed) buildings.** Small apps exist: Binayati (Lebanon), Sukkan (Jordan), Syndic Connect (Morocco), Majma'i and Khedma Complex (Arabic iOS). None shows meaningful published traction (see §4). **The white space is real, and Egypt's dense apartment-building stock makes it larger there than in the GCC (ESTIMATE).**

Note the inconsistency: REGA's end-2023 "~36,000 associations" and Al-Eqtisadiah's Sep-2024 "15,810 approved" use different definitions (registered vs approved). The article's own "69% activated" is arithmetically ~59%.

**Takeaways for Egypt**
- Even with a legal mandate, **activation is only ~60%**, and experts complain about usability. A government platform gives legitimacy but a poor experience. **There is room for a private app with a better UX that sits on top of the official process.** In Dubai, ADDA, NoBrokerHood and ISKAAN do exactly this by integrating with Mollak.
- **Features to copy:** e-voting for union leadership, digital minutes, fee billing, a bank-account letter generator, and a "manager marketplace" (618 contractable companies). That last one is a **monetization lane: the app as a marketplace for building-management and maintenance contractors.**

| Item | Fact | Source |

| Scale | 10M+ contracts since launch; 8.3M+ residential (82.3%) (REGA, 29 Sep 2024) | [REGA](https://rega.gov.sa/en/media-center/news-announcements/ejar-registers-over-10-million-rental-contracts-since-launch/) |

**Takeaway:** adoption came from three things together: **(a) legal validity, (b) a payment channel monopoly, and (c) linkage to a benefit.** A private Egyptian app cannot mandate anything. It *can* copy the "**valid receipt**" idea. Make the in-app receipt the accepted proof of payment inside the building, signed by the treasurer and visible to all residents. Social enforcement then replaces legal enforcement.

**Takeaways:**
- The **Service Charge Index** is a public benchmark of what a fair fee is per community, and it is a great borrowable idea. An Egyptian app can build a crowd-sourced **"مؤشر مصاريف الصيانة"** from anonymised data: average monthly dues by district and building type, elevator contract prices, and the doorman's (البواب) salary. This creates **SEO and PR content for free**, plus negotiation leverage for users (zero marketing budget).
- The **budget → approval → invoice → escrow** chain is the transparency model. A simplified Egyptian version: the treasurer posts the budget, residents approve by in-app vote, monthly invoices are issued automatically, and every expense is logged with a photo of the receipt.

- Mostly English-first UX with Arabic added. Egyptian colloquial Arabic and voice-first UX are missing.

### 5.3 Key strategic lessons for monetization
1. **Own demand before supply.** Munjz and others that started from the service side struggled. Justlife grew by riding Careem's existing user base. The client's plan (aggregate buildings first) is the right order.

3. **Building-level bulk deals are a structural advantage.** Examples: group tank cleaning, elevator contracts, pest control for the whole building, bulk water-filter changes. 4. **Local workforce partnerships** (UC + SMASCO) beat building everything in-house when entering a market.
5. **Exit path**: telco or super-app acquisition (e& ← ServiceMarket).

**Compliant architecture options (recommendation, ESTIMATE):**
1. **1:1 WhatsApp assistant per resident (official Cloud API, task-scoped).** For example: "ادفع الصيانة", "بلّغ عن عطل", "احجز سباك", "مين دفع؟". This is allowed because it is a business-specific bot. It mirrors DEWA Rammas.
2. **"Group mirror" pattern.** The building admin forwards or exports the group chat, or the AI reads only what users share with it. 3. **Small official API groups (≤8)** for the **building board / اتحاد** (chair, treasurer, بواب, bot). This fits the cap exactly. Use them for approvals, expense sign-offs and vendor coordination.
4. **Deep links everywhere.** Every WhatsApp message carries a wa.me / app link, so that **the WhatsApp group becomes the acquisition funnel into the app**, not its replacement.

## 11. Concrete recommendations from this angle

1. **Build the Mullak-grade governance layer for free** (union formation, voting, minutes, budget, ledger, receipts). It is the credibility moat and positions the app for the coming amendments.
2. **Approach the Ministry of Housing / NUCA with a free digital union register** pilot in one new city. If accepted, it acts like a government mandate at zero marketing cost. **ESTIMATE:** a low probability but a very high payoff. The cost is mostly founder time.
3. **Copy the Dubai Service Charge Index as a public, crowd-sourced Egyptian index.** It is shareable content for press and Facebook groups.
4. **Design the WhatsApp bot within Meta's rules**: a 1:1 task bot, board groups of ≤8, and digest messages forwarded by admins. Do not rely on a bot inside existing 50–200-member groups.
5. **Make the بواب a first-class user** (gate/visitor, deliveries, tasks). This is the Egyptian equivalent of the Gulf GateKeeper and the daily-engagement hook.
6. **Delay the services marketplace until density exists in each building or district.** Launch with **building-bulk deals** (tank cleaning, pest control, elevator contracts) before on-demand single jobs. This avoids the Munjz/B8ak trap.
7. **Bake reliability into the marketplace from day one**: neighbour ratings, a guarantee, and a human WhatsApp escalation line. This addresses Justlife's main complaints.
8. **Offer a white-label app to Egyptian developers** (Emaar One / ROSHN pattern) as the B2B revenue and distribution lane for compounds.
9. **Target Egyptian owners in the Gulf** (remote dues, bill payment, maintenance for their flat). This mirrors Dubai REST's remote bill payment. Distribute through expat community groups (zero cost).
10. **Plan the end-game options early**: a payments/telco/super-app partner or acquirer (e&/ServiceMarket, Careem/Justlife precedents).
