## 0. Executive summary (the ten findings that change the plan)

1. **Nobody knows how many bawabs Egypt has, and published figures differ 6×.** An MP on the House Manpower Committee puts the number at **about 1 million** and calls higher figures exaggerated. An academic quoted in the same article gives **6.53 million** ([Egypt Telegraph, 24 Dec 2025](https://www.egypttelegraph.com/article/206702/)). The 1970 explanatory memo of Law 68/1970 said "tens of thousands" ([lawyeregypt.net](https://lawyeregypt.net/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B3-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%B5%D9%88%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%B1%D8%B3-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%A7%D8%B5/)). **My ESTIMATE is 300k–700k residential building guards**, with 1 M as a ceiling that includes villa, shop and site guards (§1.1).
2. **Bawab cash pay sits at or below the national minimum wage, plus housing and tips.** A 2026 Cairo ad offers **EGP 6,000–7,000 basic plus a room and meals** ([Forasna](https://forasna.com/job/p/%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%B3%D9%83%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%85%D9%88%D8%B9%D8%A9-6-%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B2%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A9-441071)). The minimum wage became **EGP 8,000 on 1 Jul 2026** ([WageIndicator](https://wageindicator.org/ai/work/minimum-wage/updates/2026/minimum-wage-increased-in-egypt-from-01-july-2026-july-01-2026/)). A Heliopolis bawab told Ahram his salary "doesn't last a week" ([Ahram, 3 May 2026](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5608267.aspx)). **Even EGP 100–300 a month of extra income is a meaningful incentive for him.**
3. **Paying the bawab for property brokerage is now illegal.** Real-estate brokerage requires registration with GOEIC under Law 120/1982 as amended by Law 21/2022 and the ER amendments of 25 Jan 2026. The six-month grace period ended in **Jul 2026**. A bawab was arrested in **Apr 2026** for brokering without registration ([Almasdar](https://www.almasdar.com/169576); [Masrawy](https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_cases/details/2026/4/26/2978562/)). A lawyer quoted by Masrawy says the penalty is up to two years in prison and an EGP 50k–1M fine. **The bawab program must pay only for home services, never for rental or sale leads.** This corrects the "replace the bawab's brokerage" lever in 08 §7.
4. **The law does not provide for e-voting or electronic invitations.** ER Art. 164 requires a general-assembly (GA) invitation to be **delivered against the member's signature** and posted at the entrance. ER Art. 161 elects the board by **"direct secret ballot"**. Neither the 3 Aug 2026 draft amendments nor the 2024 explanatory memo mentions electronic means ([Youm7](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/8/3/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%B3%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%B9-%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A3%D8%AD%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%85-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%B4%D8%A3%D9%86/7501398); [Masrawy](https://www.masrawy.com/news/realestate-news/details/2026/8/3/3027283/); [El Watan](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/7609464)). **So e-voting cannot be the legal "forcing function".** The app should instead handle everything around the meeting: notice, signature sheet, quorum tracking, minutes and register filing. That forces adoption through usefulness, not through law.
5. **Votes are weighted, not one-per-flat.** ER Art. 157 bases both voting share and dues on each unit's share of the land, and counts commercial or office space at double weight. Non-payers lose their counted vote (Law Art. 81). **The app needs a weighted, arrears-aware vote tally.** Competitors assume one flat, one vote, so this is a feature they lack.
6. **Digital receipts can work as legal proof, and the platform's independence is the reason.** ER Art. 172 requires the treasurer to hand a collection receipt and keep "Schedule No. 1". Law 15/2004, its ER and Court of Cassation appeal **17689/89 JY** accept electronic records if their creation time can be verified by **an independent system controlled by neither sender nor recipient** ([Andersen, 23 Jun 2026](https://eg.andersen.com/digital-correspondence-evidence/)). A third-party platform that timestamps and hashes the receipt fits that test. **Enabler.**
7. **Very few unions are registered, and no per-governorate counts exist.** In Jan 2023 a Senate committee member said only **about 3%** of unions that should exist were registered ([Cairo24](https://www.cairo24.com/1727909)). Registration is still on paper at district "technology centres" ([Youm7, 21 Mar 2026](https://www.youm7.com/amp/2026/3/21/%D9%83%D9%8A%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%BA%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%AE%D8%B7%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A8-%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%B2/7348502)). Districts are now running pressure campaigns: Nasr City, Jun 2026, threatened "legal measures" ([El Watan](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8298565)). **A "register your union in 7 days" kit is the strongest zero-budget hook for treasurers.**
8. **Penalising non-payers inside the app has no legal basis. The legal remedies are different.** The law gives a privilege right over the unit, an urgent order for payment, a fine of EGP 10–100 a month (Art. 109), loss of the counted vote, and treats non-payment like non-payment of rent (Art. 87). Cutting off the lift is not among them. Law 49/1977 bars depriving a tenant of an existing benefit ([Youm7](https://www.youm7.com/story/2021/6/7/)). **The app should automate the legal path (notice letter, then order-for-payment pack) and must not block services.**
9. **The PDPC portal launched in October 2026, and the compliance deadline is 31 Oct 2026** ([Baker McKenzie, 7 Oct 2026](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update)). A registered DPO is mandatory for legal entities. Sources disagree on the fee table: one says records up to 100k are exempt, another says organisations start at EGP 5,000 (§6.5). **The app needs a licence before launch.**
10. **Platform-work rules under Labour Law 14/2025 have NOT been issued.** On **5 Sep 2026** the minister still called them "forthcoming" ([Dostor](https://studio.dostor.org/177608)). Decree 162/2026 covers internal work rules only. **Technicians should be onboarded as independent contractors, with transparent ratings and deactivation rules,** because the minister has flagged algorithmic bias as a target ([Masrawy, 31 Aug 2026](https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2026/8/31/3041246/)).

**ESTIMATE: residential building guards = 300k–700k.**
Reasoning:
- 08 §7 estimates **~1.1 M multi-unit buildings with 5 or more units** nationally (13.0 M households ÷ 12 units per building), built on the 2017 census count of 13.47 M residential buildings ([Amwal Al Ghad](https://amwalalghad.com/ihtg)).
- Every building with more than 6 units must have a guard room by law (ER Art. 94 in the Andersen translation: "Every building comprising more than six units must provide a room for the building guard"). Many rooms stand empty or have been converted.
- I assume 25–50% of those buildings employ a dedicated bawab. Urban walk-ups in Cairo, Giza and Alexandria are mostly covered; informal-area family blocks mostly are not. That gives **~275k–550k**.
- Compounds employ security-company guards, not bawabs: roughly 1,000–1,500 compounds × 20–80 guards ≈ 20k–120k. Adding them gives **300k–670k**. I round to **300k–700k**.
- **Confidence: low.** Validate it with 20 street-level counts (§8).

### 1.3 The bawab as informal middleman: what he brokers today

| Service the bawab brokers | Evidence | Legal status in 2026 | App implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rentals and sales ("تعب رجالة", a full month's rent; marking a flat up from EGP 8,000 to 10,000 and keeping the difference) | [Ahram](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5608267.aspx) | **Illegal unless registered** with the GOEIC brokers' register ([Almasdar](https://www.almasdar.com/169576)) | **Do not pay bawabs for property leads.** Route property leads to registered brokers or to owners directly |
| Selling residents' information ("residents' personal information becomes a source of profit") | [Ahram](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5608267.aspx) | A PDPL breach risk if the app enables it | Bawab mode must **never show residents' phone numbers**. Use masked calling or in-app chat only |
| Technician referrals, errands, car washing, groceries | Errand role ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Bawab)); technician referral commissions were not documented in any source I found (**NOT FOUND**) | Legal: ordinary service referral | **This is the legitimate commission pool for bawabs** |

**Why bawabs hold power** (according to experts quoted in Ahram): they see who comes and goes every day, residents trust them, they have no overheads, and they control keys and services. Their key asset is "early information" ([Ahram](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5608267.aspx)). **Design insight: give the bawab a legitimate, larger income stream so the illegitimate one becomes less attractive.**

### 1.5 Incentive designs: what the evidence supports

No Egyptian study measures bawab incentive schemes (**NOT FOUND**). Inference from adjacent evidence:

| Design | Evidence for | Evidence against | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Per-activation referral bonus** (paid when a building goes "Live") | Fawry shows that per-transaction agent pay works in Egypt. A bawab's pay is low (§1.2), so a small bonus matters | Gaming risk: fake accounts and fake buildings | **Use it**, but pay only after verified activation (≥ 60% of units joined and 1 dues cycle recorded) |
| **Service commission** (share of phase-2 orders in his building) | It recurs and is legal | It needs phase-2 revenue first | **Main long-term incentive** (§7.1) |
| **Wallet cashback or credit** | 61.5 M wallets and 22 M IPN customers ([Egypt Today](https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/3/149807/)) make wallet payouts easy | Many bawabs prefer cash. A wallet requires a national ID | Pay to wallet or InstaPay. Offer a cash-out via Fawry for those without a wallet |
| **Tips through the app** | Tips are already a norm ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Bawab)) | Residents may resent being nudged to tip | Make it optional, with a "thank the bawab" button and no default amount |
| **Status and recognition** ("verified bawab" badge, monthly rating) | MyGate's "MyGate-trained" social proof ([ajuniorvc](https://www.ajuniorvc.com/mygate-security-app-funding-pricing-unicorn)) | Ratings can be used to punish | Badge yes. Show ratings only to the board, not publicly |
| **Insurance or payroll help** | Bawabs lack insurance and fear dismissal ("no insurance, no syndicate", cited in 07) | — | High-value, low-cost feature (§7.1) |

### 2.4 Technicians' legal status

- **Social insurance:** Law 148/2019 Art. 2 covers "business owners and those in their position", which includes anyone running a **craft activity on their own account** ([Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/5652674)). Self-employed technicians are therefore self-insured. A platform does not become their employer just by passing leads to them.
- **Labour Law 14/2025:** in force since **1 Sep 2025**. It recognises platform-based work in general terms ([Andersen](https://eg.andersen.com/egypts-labour-law-14-2025/)), but **the platform-work ministerial decisions are still pending** (§6.4).
- **E-receipts:** I found no 2026 rule requiring individual craftsmen to issue e-receipts. The ETA head said self-employed professionals serving consumers directly are not obliged to register for e-invoicing ([search summary, KPMG/ETA](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/07/tnf-egypt-new-taxpayers-required-to-comply-with-mandate-to-issue-electronic-receipts-for-b2c-transactions.html)). **The platform's own commission revenue is subject to tax and VAT, but the individual technicians are generally not.** Confirm with a tax adviser.

## 4. Legal enablers: what the texts actually say

### 4.1 E-voting and electronic GA invitations (Law 119/2008 + ER 144/2009)

**What the texts say** (from the [Andersen translation](https://eg.andersen.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Law-No.-119-of-2008.pdf)):
- **Law Art. 84:** invitations go to all members **≥ 15 days** before the meeting, "in accordance with the ER".
- **ER Art. 164:** the invitation must state the agenda, documents, date, time, place **and the date of the second meeting**. It "shall be **delivered to members residing in the property… against their signature evidencing receipt**". Non-residents are notified at their residence or chosen domicile. The invitation is "**also announced on the notice board** at the entrance".
- **ER Art. 165:** members attend **in person**. An apology must be written. A member may appoint **only one** other member as proxy.
- **ER Art. 166:** a non-resident who has not given an address may be validly notified at the court registry.
- **ER Art. 167:** quorum is an **absolute majority**. If it is not met, the second meeting (1 hour to 15 days later) is valid with any attendance. An **attendance sheet** is required. Decisions pass by majority of those present. **Non-payers' votes are not counted.**
- **ER Art. 161:** the board is elected "**by direct secret ballot**" for 3 years.
- **ER Art. 157:** voting weight equals the unit's **land share**, with commercial or office square metres counted ×2.
- **Electronic means:** **none mentioned** in the law or ER. The 2024 and 2026 amendment coverage mentions none either (sources in §3.1).
- **Analogy:** for companies, Egypt allowed electronic GAs through GAFI Regulation 160/2020 and FRA Decree 150/2023 ([Al Tamimi](https://www.tamimi.com/news/egypts-financial-regulatory-authority-calls-for-electronic-voting-and-digitizes-services-in-the-wake-of-covid-19); [Mondaq](https://mondaq.com/shareholders/908510/holding-general-shareholders-meeting-through-electronic-means)). That required an explicit regulator instrument. **No equivalent exists for occupants' unions.**

**Verdict: BLOCKER for binding e-voting, ENABLER for "meeting ops".** A GA held only online, with invitations sent only by WhatsApp, could be challenged. Grievances are filed with the local unit (ER 160), so a disgruntled non-payer could get the decision annulled. **Hybrid design that stays within the law:**
1. The app generates the invitation PDF with every field ER 164 requires. **The bawab delivers it** and photographs the signed acknowledgement sheet in the app. This gives him a paid, legal task.
2. The app sends a WhatsApp or push copy as a courtesy notice, which also builds proof of delivery.
3. On the day, the app runs a **QR attendance sheet** and a proxy check (one proxy per member). It tallies weighted votes, excluding non-payers, and keeps a paper ballot for board elections.
4. The app produces the minutes book entry and the filing pack for the local-unit register.
5. Non-binding **"pre-polls"** in the app gather opinions between GAs. They are legal because they bind no one.

### 4.2 Digital receipts as legal proof

- **ER Art. 172** requires a "collection receipt delivered by the treasurer". It does not say the receipt must be on paper.
- **Law 15/2004 Art. 14–15** gives qualifying electronic signatures and writings evidentiary value. **ER (109/2005, amended by 361/2020) Art. 4** requires creation time to be verifiable "through an **independent preservation system not controlled by the creator or recipient**", the source to be identifiable, and automatic records to be free from alteration ([Andersen, 23 Jun 2026](https://eg.andersen.com/digital-correspondence-evidence/)).
- **Court of Cassation, appeal 17689/89 JY:** "electronic communications constitute an independent category of documentary evidence". Printouts are not inadmissible merely for lacking an original (same source; see also [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/contracts-and-commercial-law/1737494/electronic-signatures-in-egypt-%7c-legal-validity-under-law-15-of-2004)).
- **Caveat:** only a *Qualified* e-signature is accepted without extra proof. Other e-signatures need supporting evidence ([GlobalSign summary](https://www.globalsign.com/en/legality-guidelines)).

**Verdict: ENABLER, used as evidence rather than as a qualified e-signature.** Design: the receipt carries a server timestamp, a SHA-256 hash, the treasurer's identity verified by OTP, the payer's in-app confirmation, the payment-rail reference (InstaPay or wallet transaction ID), and an exportable audit log. Offer an optional QES via a licensed CA later. **Marketing line for treasurers:** "Your receipt holds up in court better than a paper receipt book."

### 4.3 Union bank, PSP or e-wallet accounts

- **Law Art. 76:** the union "**acquires juridical personality upon registration**". An unregistered union has no legal person to hold an account.
- **Deputy governor (Youm7, Oct 2023):** union formation includes opening a bank account, and a multi-building union may open a bank account to receive donations ([Youm7](https://www.youm7.com/story/2023/10/26/%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%BA%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%8A%D8%AD%D9%85%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%82%D9%88%D8%B7-%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%AD%D9%82-%D9%84%D9%87-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%B5%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89/6352079)). This is a secondary source. **Bank-specific KYC lists: NOT FOUND.**
- **Mobile wallets** are personal products. CIB's terms, for example, forbid depositing into wallets in other clients' names ([CIB T&C](https://www.cibeg.com/-/media/project/downloads/terms-and-conditions/personal-banking/cibsmart-walletterms--conditionsen.pdf)). **No CBE rule for "union" or association wallets was found.**
- A platform that **holds** residents' money needs a CBE licence (see 09 for the PSP framework). It must **route** payments through a licensed PSP instead.

**Verdict: PARTIAL ENABLER.** Registered unions can likely hold a bank account, and through it a PSP merchant account. Unregistered buildings (about 97%) cannot. **Design:**
- Tier A (unregistered): residents pay the treasurer's personal InstaPay or wallet through a payment link. The app reconciles the payment and issues the receipt. Funds stay in the treasurer's account, so show a "commingling" warning and a monthly statement.
- Tier B (registered): a union bank account linked to a PSP collection link with automatic reconciliation.
- **Use the gap as a growth hook:** "Register your union to unlock a union account". This pushes treasurers into the legal toolkit (§7.3).

### 4.4 Restrictions on non-payers enforced by the app

- **What the law gives** (Law 119): no counted vote (Art. 81); **privilege right** over the unit and movables, registration free of fees (Art. 87); **urgent order for payment** from the summary-matters judge after notice (Art. 87); non-payment has "**the same legal effects as failure to pay rent**" (Art. 87); a criminal fine of **EGP 10–100 per month** of default plus an order to pay (Art. 109).
- **What it does not give:** any right to cut lifts, water or keys. Law 49/1977 forbids depriving a tenant of an existing benefit, and a summary judge can order it restored ([Youm7, 2021](https://www.youm7.com/story/2021/6/7/)). A Moroccan court in Apr 2026 ordered lift access restored for an owners' association member in arrears. That case is not Egyptian law, but it shows the direction courts take ([search summary, al3omk](https://al3omk.com/?p=1164659)).
- **PDPL risk:** posting a named list of non-payers to the whole building discloses personal financial data and may also be defamatory.

**Verdict: BLOCKER for service cut-offs, ENABLER for legal collection.** Design: a 3-step escalation:
1. A friendly reminder.
2. A formal notice letter, generated in the legal form and delivered by the bawab or by registered mail.
3. An "order-for-payment pack" with the arrears statement from the ledger, GA decision extracts and receipts, ready for a lawyer.

Residents see the building's collection rate in aggregate only. Names go to the board only.

### 4.6 Social insurance and the legal employer of building guards

- **NOSI:** building guards and gardeners **are covered** by the Social Insurance Law, provided the workplace is not a private dwelling ([El Watan, 2021](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/5801701)).
- **Who employs the bawab:** Law 68/1970 Art. 1 says it is whoever assigns him: the **owner, occupant, tenant, operator or manager**. ER Art. 168 lets the **GA appoint, dismiss and set the wages of guards only where all members are owners**. Otherwise the owner or landlord keeps that power. ER Art. 171 lists "security costs" as a fixed union expense.
- Law 148/2019 obliges the employer to enrol the worker and pay contributions ([Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/5652674)).
- The bill making a social-insurance certificate a licensing condition is **not confirmed as passed** (§1.2).

**Verdict: ENABLER for a "bawab payroll" feature.** Usually the union (in an all-owner building) or the owner is the employer. The app can split the bawab's salary across units by land share (ER 157), record payments, generate a work contract template, and guide the NOSI enrolment (documents listed by El Watan and [Youm7, Sep 2026](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/10/%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%AF%D9%85%D9%87%D8%A7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%BA%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%B8%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%87%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%A3%D9%85%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B1%D9%81-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%87%D8%A7/7538741)). ## 6. Product and growth recommendations

### 6.1 Bawab program ("برنامج حارس العمارة")

**Positioning:** "Your building's bawab gets a salary record, insurance help and extra income, legally."

| Element | Design | Cost / funding |
|---|---|---|
| **Bawab Mode app** | Voice-first Android. 4 big buttons: requests, deliveries, invites/signatures, my income. Arabic voice notes are transcribed by AI. Works on a low-end phone, with data-lite offline queues | Dev only |
| **Onboarding** | A 15-minute in-person session by the "building captain" or treasurer, with a printed one-pager. MyGate needed 4–6 days because its guards ran the gates; our scope is smaller | Zero cash |
| **Activation bonus** | EGP 100 per building that reaches "Live" (≥ 60% of units, 1 dues cycle). Paid by InstaPay or wallet, with a Fawry cash-out alternative | ESTIMATE EGP 100 × 2–5k buildings in year 1 = EGP 200k–500k. **Can be funded by deferring it**: "bonus credited when phase-2 services launch" |
| **Legal paid tasks** | Deliver GA invitations and capture signatures (ER 164); deliver notice letters; confirm technician arrival. Paid by the union from its budget at a rate the GA sets (ER 164 allows paid helpers with GA approval) | Zero for us |
| **Service commission (phase 2)** | 2–3% of GMV of services ordered in his building (or 20% of our take). **Never on rentals or sales** | Funded by revenue |
| **Payroll + insurance** | Salary split by land share, digital payslip, NOSI checklist, contract template | Dev only |
| **Guardrails** | Never show residents' phone numbers; masked chat; resident rating visible only to the board; misconduct reporting; program terms ban property brokerage and data sale | — |
| **Bawab-to-bawab referral** | EGP 50 when a referred bawab's building goes Live. Uses the village networks ([Ahram](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5608267.aspx)) | ESTIMATE ≤ EGP 100k year 1 |

**Why it works:** the bawab gains documentation (salary, insurance), legal income (signature runs, service commissions) and status, in exchange for being the building's onboarding desk.

### 6.2 Technician onboarding

1. **Seed from the bawab and the treasurer.** Ask each Live building: "Who is your plumber, electrician, lift company?" Invite them with a WhatsApp link, giving 3–5 vetted pros per building at zero acquisition cost.
2. **Verification:** national ID, OCR'd by AI; a selfie; 2 building references; a portfolio of photos; an optional 10-minute video skills check.
3. **Economics:** **free for the first 6 months**, then 10–15% or a flat lead fee. Collect cash or InstaPay through payment links to limit leakage. Pay weekly.
4. **Retention lever:** "The building's preferred plumber" badge, earned through building ratings, plus repeat orders from the same building.
5. **Rules:** published rate cards for common jobs. Disputes go through photo evidence and an appeal. Deactivation needs 2 documented incidents. This anticipates the platform-work decisions.
6. **Do not** require exclusivity, uniforms or shift schedules. Keep them as contractors.
