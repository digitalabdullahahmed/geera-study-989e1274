# 07: Voice of the Customer: What Egyptians Say About Life in Buildings and Compounds

**Research angle:** Social and public voice (Facebook, X, Reddit, forums, Egyptian news, app-store reviews). Covers Egyptian building and compound pain points and what residents ask for.
**Date of research:** 2026-10-12
**Market focus:** Egypt only. Regional or foreign sources are used only for contrast and are labelled as such.
**Prepared for:** A founder building a free, mass-adoption building and compound super-app (phase 1) that later becomes a services marketplace / intermediary (phase 2).

---

## 0. Executive summary (TL;DR)

1. **Money is the main pain: collecting dues and splitting them fairly.** Egyptian legal commentators, news columnists and housing-ministry drafts all say **collecting dues is "the biggest problem facing occupants' unions"** ([Al-Ahram, 2019](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/2139186.aspx)). Non-payers, unequal shares (ground floor vs. top floor, apartments still being finished, exempt units) and "a few residents carry the burden" come up constantly ([Official Egyptian Real Estate Platform blog, 2025](https://blogs.realestate.gov.eg/ar/cost-of-property-maintenance-in-egypt-monthly-breakdown/)). The newest Egyptian building-fund apps get reviews that ask for **custom per-unit splits, exemptions and one-off charges** ([Play Store reviews, 2026](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.amarati.app)).
2. **Spending transparency and trust in whoever holds the money (union treasurer, developer, management company) is the second-largest pain.** Columnists describe forged general-assembly minutes, treasurers who can't keep accounts, and maintenance deposits that act as "a big door to unregulated corruption" ([Youm7, 2022](https://www.youm7.com/story/2022/6/9/%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A9-%D9%88%D9%82%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A8-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%A9/5794389)). Residents allege a security contractor is "paid for 30 staff but only 6 are on site" ([Al-Wafd, Oct 2026](https://www.alwafd.news/5975683)).
3. **Compound-versus-management-company conflict is getting worse in 2026.** Examples include a two-month water cut affecting 500 families in a Sheikh Zayed compound after a fee dispute ([Cairo24, Jul 2026](https://www.cairo24.com/2464858)), a physical fight over a gate in a New Cairo compound that went to prosecutors ([Youm7, Jun 2026](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/6/12/%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%8A-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D8%B1%D8%A8-%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%B3%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%83%D9%85%D8%A8%D9%88%D9%86%D8%AF-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A8-%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%A9/7446216)), and an op-ed on the "hidden governance problem" of gated communities ([Amwal Al Ghad, Jul 2026](https://en.amwalalghad.com/the-hidden-governance-problem-inside-egypts-gated-communities/)).
4. **Finding a trustworthy technician is still unsolved, and the leading app has damaged trust.** FilKhedma (100k+ installs) has a **3.07★ rating**. In our sample of its 200 most recent reviews, 45% were 1–2★. The recurring themes are overcharging, unskilled technicians, no-shows, customer service that doesn't answer, and outright "scam/theft" accusations ([Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.filkhedma.customer)). This gap is the phase-2 monetization opportunity.
5. **The bawab (doorman) already works as an informal broker.** Ahram (May 2026) reports that the bawab "has become a key link in a parallel property market, working as an intermediary and information collector", charging commissions and selling residents' information ([Ahram Gate](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5608267.aspx)). **This shows Egyptians already pay a building-level intermediary.** The app can formalize that role and capture it, ideally with the bawab as a partner rather than an enemy.
6. **Elevator safety is a life-and-death issue that gets national attention.** Repeated fatal elevator falls have led to parliamentary briefing requests ([Parlmany](https://www.parlmany.com/News/2/479428/حوادث-الأسانسير-أمام-البرلمان-نواب-يطالبون-بتفعيل-قانون-119-بشأن)) and a March 2026 Akhbar El-Yom investigation titled "Elevator disasters... a crime of negligence" ([Akhbar El-Yom](https://akhbarelyom.com/news/newdetails/4792651/1/عشرات-الحوادث-كوارث-الأسانسير-جريمة-إهما)). Unpaid fees lead to missed maintenance, and missed maintenance leads to accidents.
7. **Utilities cause anger every month.** The issues are prepaid electricity card balances that "evaporate" ([Bawabat Al-Omal, Aug 2026](https://elommal.com/archives/380038); [Youm7, Aug 2026](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/8/30/%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B0%D8%A7-%D9%8A%D8%AE%D8%AA%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%B1%D8%B5%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A8%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%AD%D9%86-%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B8-%D8%B3%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%88%D9%89-%D9%8A%D9%88%D8%B6%D8%AD/7531036)), water cuts that run past their announced time (Giza 2025, 18h instead of 8h, [Al-Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/5524489)), and a cleaning fee on the electricity bill that people feel they pay "twice" ([Safha1, Feb 2026](https://www.safha1.com/12157)).
8. **Existing compound and developer apps are hated for basic failures, not for missing features.** Across six developer and compound apps (Emaar Misr, Palm Hills x2, Mountain View, TMG Life, Ahl Madinaty), 115 negative reviews are dominated by crashes, slowness and "security check failed" (≈43%) and by login, OTP and registration failures (≈22%). One review reads: "You are now blocked due to violation of the single device role, Please visit your nearest office" ([Mountain View Community](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.congora.mountainview)). **Making onboarding and access reliable is a competitive advantage in itself.**
9. **A direct competitor already pitches "not every resident needs to install" plus an AI assistant.** A 5★ review of *Ettihad* (Aug 2026) says: "فكرة اني مش لازم كل السكان ينزل التطبيق ممتازة. وفاري الذكاء الاصطناعي حلم" ([Ettihad](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ittihad.ettihad)). [corrected: Ettihad's 4.5★ rests on only **8 ratings** (≈3k installs, released 10 Aug 2026), so treat it as an early anecdote, not proof of traction.] This supports the founder's WhatsApp-first and AI ideas, and it also means the window to differentiate is closing.
10. **Source-access limitation:** Reddit, Facebook groups and X could **not** be read directly. Reddit blocked both search and fetch, and Facebook and X content is not indexed in a form we could reach. The Reddit/FB/X voice is therefore represented **indirectly**: news articles that quote Facebook groups and viral posts, and **≈560 Google Play reviews pulled directly** from 16 Egyptian building, compound and home-service apps. See §2 and §9 for the manual follow-up research we recommend.

## 3. Deep dives: pain points with verbatim evidence

### P1. Non-payment of maintenance dues and unfair splitting (Evidence: A)

**What people say**

- Al-Ahram (legal consultant Mahmoud Enany): **"أكبر مشكلة تواجه اتحاد الشاغلين تحصيل الاشتراكات"** ("the biggest problem facing occupants' unions is collecting dues"). Source: [gate.ahram.org.eg/News/2139186.aspx](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/2139186.aspx) (Apr 2019).
- The Official Egyptian Real Estate Platform blog (Sep 2025) lists owner complaints: owners who refuse or delay payment "especially when cost distribution is not transparent", **"a few residents carry most of the burden"**, haphazard collection in popular areas, and disputes between tenants and owners over who pays. Typical cost: **200–600 EGP/month per unit** in mid-range buildings and **1,000–3,000 EGP/month** in upscale compounds ([blogs.realestate.gov.eg](https://blogs.realestate.gov.eg/ar/cost-of-property-maintenance-in-egypt-monthly-breakdown/)).
- Banker.news (Sep 2026), expert Tarek Shoukry: **"امتناع بعض الملاك عن سداد رسوم الصيانة أو التأخر في سدادها"** is "one of the most prominent challenges". Management companies "face delays in collecting dues from some owners" ([banker.news/154253](https://banker.news/154253)).
- NYT/Asharq Al-Awsat on Cairo's old elevators: "tenant squabbles over maintenance fees that sometimes turn so petty" and **"residents who do pay install key-fob systems to condemn nonpayers to the stairs"** ([Asharq Al-Awsat English, Sep 2021](https://english.aawsat.com/home/article/3205346/cairo%E2%80%99s-antique-elevators-glorious-and-glitchy-are-scenes-love-and-fear)).
- Al-Dostor: an expert calls for a legal mechanism to penalize non-payers ([dostor.org/3483595](https://www.dostor.org/3483595), snippet).
- Saad Fathy legal blog: unions "struggled to collect monthly maintenance subscriptions, since no legal power let it compel owners to pay" ([saadfathy.com](https://saadfathy.com/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D9%88%D8%B6%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%BA%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%86/), snippet).
- Youm7 (older explainer): neighbour disputes in a single building **"increased because of the disagreement on the water bill and how to divide it among units"** ([Youm7, 2018](https://www.youm7.com/story/2018/9/15/%D8%B4%D8%A7%D9%87%D8%AF-%D9%81%D9%89-%D8%AF%D9%82%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%A9-9-%D8%AE%D8%B7%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%83%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%87-%D9%85%D9%86%D9%81%D8%B5%D9%84-%D9%84%D8%B4%D9%82%D8%AA%D9%83/3950378), snippet).
- The legal penalty is weak. The current law and the drafts discussed set a fine of **10–100 EGP per month of delay** ([Al-Ahram](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/2139186.aspx); [Invest-Gate 2021](https://invest-gate.me/features/management-dilemma-occupants-union-or-developer-management/)). The 2024 draft amendment would allow **administrative seizure (الحجز الإداري)** of property to recover dues where management companies are appointed ([El-Watan, Oct 2024](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/7609464)).

**Play Store voices (the people actually managing building funds, 2026):**

**Product implications**
- A **split engine** covering equal shares, by area (m²), by floor, by usage, exemptions, temporary zero, one-off levies and per-service opt-in. This is the most-requested feature in competitor reviews.
- **Admin can register on behalf of residents** (elderly, non-smartphone). This supports the founder's "every resident has an account" goal without forcing installs (see §5).
- **Soft-enforcement mechanics** with no legal risk: automated polite reminders, monthly "building health" score, opt-in public paid-list, and later digital elevator access (an echo of the key-fob practice). **Caution:** a Moroccan court barred depriving non-payers of the elevator and parking ([Hespress FR](https://fr.hespress.com/475788-copropriete-la-justice-interdit-de-priver-un-proprietaire-dascenseur-et-de-parking-pour-impayes.html)). Egyptian legal review is needed before any feature restricts access.

### P2. Transparency of spending and trust in whoever holds the money (Evidence: A)

- Al-Ahram (2019) lists frequent complaints of **"وقائع تزوير اجتماعات الجمعيات العمومية ومحاضر مجالس الإدارة"** (forgery of general assembly meetings and board minutes), treasurers lacking basic accounting skills, and union funds exposed to risk. It proposes treating union funds as public funds ([Ahram](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/2139186.aspx)).
- Akhbar El-Yom, "مأساة اتحادات الشاغلين": there is no state oversight, and **general assemblies with very few attendees can impose financial obligations on everyone** ([Akhbar El-Yom](https://akhbarelyom.com/news/newdetails/4808370/1/مأساة-اتحادات-الشاغلين), snippet; page returned 403).
- Youm7 column, "وديعة الصيانة.. وقواعد النصب بشياكة" (Jun 2022): the maintenance deposit is **"باب كبير لفساد غير مقنن"**, used to hire staff and even to build **"محال تجارية ومولات ومدارس … على حساب وديعة الصيانة"** ([Youm7](https://www.youm7.com/story/2022/6/9/%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A9-%D9%88%D9%82%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A8-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%A9/5794389)).
- Banker.news (Sep 2026): the expert Mohamed Khattab says **"العميل من حقه معرفة كيفية إنفاق عوائد أموال الصيانة"**. Experts call for separate maintenance accounts, an independent auditor, and a closing budget audited by a certified accountant and presented to the union AGM ([banker.news/154253](https://banker.news/154253)).
- Amwal Al Ghad op-ed (Jul 2026): buyers face "recurring increases years later … **without a clear explanation of how those increases were calculated**". It recommends "separate bank accounts, annual budgets, independent financial audits, full disclosure of major contracts" ([Amwal Al Ghad](https://en.amwalalghad.com/the-hidden-governance-problem-inside-egypts-gated-communities/)).
- Al-Wafd (Oct 2026), Paradise compound: residents claim **"مقاول الأمن يتقاضى مستحقات مقابل تشغيل 30 فرداً، بينما لا يتواجد على أرض الواقع سوى 6 أفراد فقط"** ([Al-Wafd](https://www.alwafd.news/5975683)).
- Elbalad (2017), Madinaty: residents report that security costs 60M EGP while **"أبواب المدينة مفتوحة لكل من هب ودب"**, and that "معظم لمبات الحدائق لا تعمل". The electricity regulator found meters charged at up to 10,000 EGP against a ≈2,300 EGP average cost ([Elbalad](https://www.elbalad.news/2926838)).
- Reviewers name transparency explicitly as a reason they love the new fund apps: "يجمع بين سهولة الاستخدام والدقة والتنظيم والشفافية" ([Sandooq El-Emara](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.amrem.sandooqelamara), 2026-09-29).

**Product implications:** an **expense feed where every outflow needs a photo receipt**, a vendor-quote comparison (three quotes) for large items, **e-voting with quorum tracking**, immutable edit history ("who changed what"), and a one-tap **monthly report to every resident** (PDF + WhatsApp). Transparency is what turns a resident from someone who refuses to pay into a payer. It is also the trust layer that the phase-2 marketplace depends on.

**Implications for phase 2 (the monetization core):**
1. **Building-level reputation**: "3 neighbours in your building used this plumber, 4.8★". This is the trust primitive FilKhedma lacks, and only a building-network app has it.
2. **Price transparency**: fixed menu prices for common jobs, a "كشف" (inspection) fee stated up front, parts at receipt price with a photo, and an AI quote-checker ("Is 900 EGP fair to change a water-heater element in Nasr City?").
3. **Fair take rate**: technicians publicly resent 30%. A lower commission plus a subscription or lead fee reduces off-platform leakage (ESTIMATE: 10–15% take rate or flat lead fee, to be validated).
4. **Warranty and escrow**: hold payment until the resident confirms, with a 7–30 day re-visit guarantee.
5. **Use local supply**: the building's own bawab-recommended technician can onboard, which turns the informal network into platform supply.

### P13. WhatsApp-group chaos (Evidence: C, mostly inferred)

- **No Egyptian article or forum post specifically about building WhatsApp-group chaos was found** in these searches. Reddit, Facebook and X were inaccessible. We do not claim quotes we don't have.
- Indirect evidence:
  - Building WhatsApp groups clearly exist and serve as the alert channel. In an Alexandria criminal case, neighbours "wrote about a foul smell on the building's WhatsApp group" ([Cairo24](https://www.cairo24.com/2465466), snippet).
  - Egyptian governments and utilities run complaints through WhatsApp: Cairo Governorate has a WhatsApp complaints line, and the water company accepts complaints by WhatsApp ([Ahram](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/2286455.aspx); [El-Watan](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/7016116)). WhatsApp is the default civic channel.
  - Egyptian building-fund apps already push **monthly receipts via WhatsApp** ([Nafezly portfolio](https://nafezly.com/portfolio/91389-%D8%A8%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%AC-%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%8F%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%83-%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%83)).
  - **The Ettihad review praising "not every resident has to install the app" plus the AI assistant** ([Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ittihad.ettihad), 2026-08-28) shows the market rewards a meet-them-where-they-are approach.
  - Foreign analogues: a South African estate feud "played out on the WhatsApp group, with each party trying to score points" ([PrivateProperty](https://www.privateproperty.co.za/advice/lifestyle/articles/golden-rules-for-whatsapp-communities/7199)), and a UK parent saying a street group "gives me anxiety!" ([Mumsnet](https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/_chat/5047411-tell-me-about-street-whatsapp-group)).
- **ESTIMATE (reasoned):** almost every Egyptian building with an active union head runs a WhatsApp group. Typical failure modes include lost announcements, money discussed publicly (shaming non-payers), arguments, spam and forwarded rumours, and no record of decisions. **Validation needed:** a 5-minute survey in 20–30 building groups (see §8).

**Implication for the founder's WhatsApp-bot idea:** the bot should **reduce noise, not add to it**. It should be silent by default and respond only when @mentioned or to commands like "سجل إن شقة 5 دفعت". It turns free text into structured records (payments, complaints, votes), posts a **weekly digest**, and moves sensitive topics (who hasn't paid) into private 1:1 messages. **Note [corrected]:** the WhatsApp Cloud API cannot join or read an *existing* consumer group the way a human member does. Since 2025 Meta offers a **Groups API**, but only to Official Business Accounts (green-tick). It works only for groups the business itself creates, residents join through an invite link, and each group holds **at most 8 participants**, which is too small for a whole building ([Meta docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/groups/)). Unofficial "bot joins the group" tools rely on non-API automation that breaks WhatsApp terms and risks a number ban. Feasibility is covered in the tech/AI research doc. A practical design is a bot number that residents add, or a "Communities" approach plus 1:1 bot chats.

## 6. Phase-2 (intermediary) monetization signals from the VOC

1. **Willingness to pay an intermediary already exists.** Bawabs charge commissions to give out an owner's number or close a rental ([Ahram 2026](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5608267.aspx)). Residents also pay private zabbals on top of official fees ([Safha1](https://www.safha1.com/12157)).
2. **The leading pure marketplace has failed on trust**: FilKhedma at 3.07★. Technicians also hate its 30% cut, and that cut fuels off-platform deals. **A building-anchored marketplace** with neighbour ratings and union-level purchasing can beat it on trust and leakage.
3. **High-value recurring B2B2C contracts**: elevator maintenance (legally mandatory and widely ignored), security contractors (the "30 paid vs. 6 present" scandal), cleaning, tank cleaning, and pest control. These are bought **by the union** with pooled money, and the app already sees that budget.
4. **Utility payments**: monthly frequency and pain over hidden fees (15–20 EGP illegal shop fees). A transparent, low-fee charging channel is a habit hook. A small convenience fee needs a licensed partner (ESTIMATE).
5. **"Managed union" service** for buildings that want a professional manager without paying a 1–2M EGP/month Class-1 firm (residents' estimate) ([Al-Wafd](https://www.alwafd.news/5975683)). A part-time remote accountant plus app automation could sell at a tiny fraction of that (ESTIMATE; to be priced in the feasibility doc).
