Recommended identity · for founder review · 12 Oct 2026

The lit open door. Every building has one, and someone is always home.

جيرة… حق الجيرة بقى أسهل

Geera's mark is the entrance of an Egyptian عمارة: a round arch, the door left open for the neighbours, and the stairwell light switched on. That light is Sanad, the assistant. The logo and the AI are the same object.

Built on C4, judge score 220 Every judge weakness fixed Flat colour, no gradients

The story in one breath

A bawab or a 70-year-old owner can describe it without reading a word:

«الأبلكيشن الأزرق اللي فيه باب مفتوح والنور والع»

“The blue app with the open door and the light on.”

The arch

The building entrance, مدخل العمارة. Round and domestic, never pointed, so it never reads as a mihrab. It fits a Shubra stairwell and a New Cairo gate.

The open leaf

حق الجيرة: the door is open to neighbours. It also means the ledger is open and nothing is hidden.

The light

Sanad. The one light in the stairwell that is always on. Give it a face and it becomes the assistant's avatar.

Why it wins

  • It is a picture, not a letter. Anyone can name a door, whatever their literacy. The jeem monograms in C2 and C4 read as a Latin C or E to many people.
  • Lapis blue means protection here. The خرزة زرقا blue, moved away from InstaPay purple (ΔE 28) and clear of WhatsApp green (ΔE 156).
  • Money screens stay calm. Ledgers sit on deep ink with white tabular numbers. Brand colours never mean paid or due.
  • Status colours survive colour blindness. Paid, Due and Overdue differ in lightness and fill style, with a minimum separation of ΔE 22 under protanopia. Icon plus word is always required.
  • Holds up at 16 px and on a sticker. A dedicated small symbol, a one-colour version for receipts and stamps, and no gradients anywhere.
  • The AI cue is ours. No generic sparkle. When Sanad acts, you see the lit doorway with a face.

Sizes and clear space

Below 32 px the small symbol takes over: a 17-unit frame instead of 13 and a straight door leaf. The lamp light stays visible down to 16 px.

Clear space is X, one frame thickness (13 of 100 units), on every side of the symbol, and 1.5 X around lockups. Nothing enters it, including WhatsApp chrome.

Don’t

Mirror the door. The leaf always hangs on the left jamb.

Blue on a yellow field. That is Fawry’s territory.

Recolour the light. It is always lamp gold.

Add glows or shadows. Flat colour only.

Close the door. The leaf never covers the opening.

C1 · Al-Bab الباب

Modern Egyptian heritage
218 pts

A Cairene keel arch holding a mashrabiya of four windows, one lit. Clay, sand and Nile ink.

Clay #B5532FNile ink #1F2A44Sand #D9A441Plaster #F7F1E8Bead #34589A
Why it lost
  • The pointed keel arch can read as a mihrab or mosque front.
  • Clay primary sat next to the Overdue red, so the brand colour competed with “late”.
  • Four lattice diamonds blur together at 16 to 24 px.

Kept in the final: the lit window as the shared signature, Sanad as the mark with a face, and the hairline-cut mono version.

C2 · Ink & Lantern حبر وفانوس

Trust-first fintech
213 pts

A geometric jeem whose dot is a lit window and whose base is the ledger’s total line. Deep ink with apricot.

Ink #1B1F4BLantern #FFB37CDusk #4B4F86Plaster #F5EEE4Mist #E8E9F4
Why it lost
  • Due and Overdue were nearly identical under deuteranopia (ΔE 2).
  • The ink tile vanished on a dark launcher (1.21:1).
  • A letter monogram asks people to read before they recognise.

Kept in the final: ink money surfaces, the inverted dark-mode button, a 3:1 input border, and Sanad’s «صباح الفل» voice.

→

C4 · Lapis Spark, as submitted and as refined 220 pts

The winner had the strongest idea (lapis and gold, protected and precious) but carried fixable faults: electric violet close to InstaPay, a Gemini-style sparkle, a jeem that read as a Latin C, gradients that bloom on cheap screens, and a rose glow near the dark-mode Overdue pink. The recommendation keeps the pairing and replaces the rest with the lit door.

Before we lock it

Next steps الخطوة الجاية

The identity is ready to use in prototypes now. These checks come before printing anything or paying for the Play listing.

  1. Trademark search for “Geera” and «جيرة» in Egypt

    Search the word marks in Latin and Arabic script at EGIPO (Egyptian trademark office), and a figurative search for “open arched door” marks. Classes to cover: 9 (app), 36 (collecting dues), 42 (software service) and 45 if community services grow.

    EGIPOWord + figurativeClasses 9 · 36 · 42

  2. Domain and handle check

    Check geera.app, geera.eg / geera.com.eg and the short link geera.link used on the share cards. Reserve matching handles for the WhatsApp Business display name «سند · جيرة», Instagram and Facebook.

    geera.appgeera.com.eggeera.linkSocial handles

  3. Have a type designer outline the Arabic wordmark

    Today’s outlines come straight from Baloo Bhaijaan 2 (OFL allows modification). A type designer should hand-tune the «جيرة» joins, the “g” descender and the match between the two scripts, then deliver final outlines.

    JoinsDescenderOptical balance

  4. Test with 5 to 10 real people

    A bawab, an elderly owner, a treasurer and a compound resident. Can they describe the icon in one breath? Does anyone read the arch as religious or as a tombstone?

  5. Check colours on two cheap Android screens

    Lapis #3640BE and the lamp light at minimum brightness, on low-end LCDs, in sunlight and at night.
